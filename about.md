  <section class="about-hero">
    <h1>About</h1>

    <p class="about-lead">
      I am a Ph.D. student in Mechatronics, Robotics, and Automation Engineering at Oregon State University and a graduate research assistant in the Manufacturing Process Control Laboratory.
    </p>

    <p>
      My work is at the intersection of control systems, precision mechatronics, and advanced manufacturing. I am particularly interested in modeling, analyzing, and controlling dynamic systems where mechanical behavior, sensing, actuation, and computation must work together reliably.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="about-grid">

    <div class="about-card">
      <h2>Research Background</h2>

      <p>
        During my master's research at Oregon State University, I worked on feed-modulated milling for chatter avoidance. This work involved modeling machining dynamics, analyzing process stability, and studying how low-frequency feed modulation can be used to influence cutting-edge engagement and suppress undesirable vibration.
      </p>

      <p>
        Before joining Oregon State University, I completed my bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Tehran. My undergraduate thesis focused on the precision positioning of a planar voice-coil actuator using linear controllers and friction compensation.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="about-card">
      <h2>Current Research Interests</h2>

      <ul>
        <li>Learning-based control for repetitive manufacturing processes</li>

        <li>Precision motion control and feed-drive systems</li>

        <li>Advanced manufacturing process control</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

  <section class="about-card wide-card">
    <h2>Technical Experience</h2>

    <p>
      I have experience with modeling, simulation, experimental system identification, and control implementation. My work has included machining-process simulation, feed-drive dynamics identification, friction compensation, cascade control, adaptive control algorithms, and real-time implementation using Simulink and dSPACE.
    </p>

    <p>
      I also have hands-on experience with CNC machines, embedded systems, instrumentation, actuator-control experiments, microcontroller programming, and experimental setup development.
    </p>
